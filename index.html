A Process Made for Busy Owners

What it's like to work with us

The core of what we do is make complex, cumbersome building projects enjoyable and manageable, specifically for busy people who have many other things to think about.

Step 1: Design & Budget

You can bring in a few ideas or a napkin sketch, we'll help your vision come to life with a full 3D rendering, covering every detail to set an accurate, achievable budget.

Step 2: Permits, Site Prep & Foundation

Acquiring permits, excavation and site preparation for the foundation utilities.

Step 3: Framing & Dry-In

Timber frames, trusses, roof, and windows highlight the scale and geometry of the building.

Step 4: Selections & Finishes

Siding, roofing, mechanicals, drywall, cabinetry, trim chosen one by one.

Step 5: Finishing Touches & Move-In

Final details checked and the keys handed over.
