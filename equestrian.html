Planning your equestrian build

Five decisions to make early

The biggest choices come early in the process. Here are some questions worth asking at each stage, before anything is set in stone.

  1. 01

    Before you Break Ground

    Make sure the site can support the barn you have in mind - drainage, trailer access, light and airflow all depend on where the barn sits.

    Questions to answer

    • Where will a future arena, wing, or paddock go?
  2. 02

    Design around the Operation

    A floor plan should follow your routine, with clear routes so horses, people and equipment don’t compete for the same space.

    Questions to answer

    • How do you want a typical day in your barn to flow?
    • What kind of space and separation do your horses need in their stalls?
  3. 03

    Build Health and Safety into the Facility

    Ventilation and fire prevention are important considerations to map out to insure safety when it matters most.

    Questions to answer

    • How will fresh air be moved through the stalls?
    • What route would horses and people use to exit in an emergency?
  4. 04

    Choose the Right Structural System

    The function of a space will help determine which structural systems is right for your barn

    Questions to answer

    • Does your barn need divided rooms or open, column-free space?
    • How should your roof and structural systems work together to handle your building’s use and changing weather conditions?
  5. 05

    Plan the Details you’ll Live with Every Day

    Daily efficiency comes down to distance and interruption. Water, power and storage should follow your routine, not force it to adapt to the building.

    Questions to answer

    • Where will you reach for water and power during chores?
    • How will bedding come in and waste go out without crossing horse traffic?
