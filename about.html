Our Core Values

The Standards we hold to

Every person on each Avodah jobsite is held to these core values, whether they’re on our payroll, a trade partner’s, or our client.

Work

How we treat the job.

Dependable
We will be there for each other, and show up on time.
Organized & Thorough
We are clean, tidy, organized, and we put things back where they belong.
Committed
We are committed to a cause larger than ourselves.

Worship

How we carry ourselves.

Transparency
We will walk with absolute transparency in every aspect of our lives
Confident Humility
We will humbly maximize the talents that we were blessed with.
Integrity
We will do what we say.

Service

How we treat people.

Outward Focus
We will focus outward on others, and not inward on ourselves.
Kindness
We will be kind in all we do, even when the kind thing is the hard thing.
Excellence
We will focus on doing the right thing in every detail.

Representing Avodah

A Consistent Team you can trust

The quality of your project depends on the people behind it. We work with the same trusted crews and trade partners because consistency leads to better results. It shows up in the details that matter—from craftsmanship that exceeds expectations to honest communication, clean jobsites, and taking responsibility when something doesn’t go as planned. Those aren’t just values we talk about—they’re the standards we bring to every project.

Our Team & Partners

Who You will see at your job site

We believe confidence comes from knowing who’s on your project and knowing they’ll be there from start to finish.

Your Project Manager

One person owns your build from the first conversation to the final walkthrough. Same name, same phone number, month one till move-in.

Our In-House Crews

We keep the stages that have the greatest impact on quality in-house instead of subcontracting them, giving us complete confidence in the craftsmanship and allowing us to stand behind the work we deliver.

A Small Set of Trade Partners

Electricians, plumbers, and specialists we’ve worked with for years and can vouch for by name, not just whoever was cheapest and available that week.

Intentionally Local

Areas we Serve

We are located on East Lincoln Way in Wooster, and we work within roughly a 50-mile radius — Wayne, Holmes, Ashland, Medina, and Stark counties and the surrounding area. We stay inside a radius we can actually service well because we believe a project manager who can be on your site the same morning you call is worth more than a wide map.
